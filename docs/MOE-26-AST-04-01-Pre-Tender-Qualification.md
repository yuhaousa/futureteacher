## OBJECTIVE

2. MOE is procuring a production-ready, commercial Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) learning platform to support educators' continuous professional learning. The platform is intended to operate at scale across schools and HQ divisions, supporting diverse learning needs and contexts. It should support diverse learning modes, including independent learning, collaborative learning, and workplace-based learning.

3. The platform should demonstrate strong out-of-the-box capabilities aligned to user needs, with configuration preferred over customisation. This reflects MOE's intent, in this context, to leverage mature, purpose-built SaaS solutions that benefit from continuous innovation and scalable architectures — allowing ongoing improvements without the cost and complexity of bespoke system development.

4. The platform should be Artificial Intelligence (AI)-ready, reflecting the importance of personalisation and knowledge discovery in professional learning. Suppliers should demonstrate how artificial intelligence is meaningfully embedded within core product features and user workflows to enhance educators' learning outcomes.

5. Solutions should have demonstrated deployment in comparable environments and be deployable with minimal implementation overhead to enable early realisation of value.

6. This will be a contract for 3 years, with extension options for up to a further 3 years, subject to terms to be agreed. Extension periods and terms will be determined based on performance, budget availability, and operational requirements.

7. This is the pre-tender qualification stage of a two-stage Selective Tender. Up to 4 suppliers who meet the qualification criteria and score highest will be invited to participate in the subsequent tender stage.

---

## CONTEXT

8. Professional learning for educators is increasingly shifting towards flexible, personalised, and practice-embedded models. Teachers today engage in learning through a combination of approaches:

(a) **Self-directed and collaborative learning:** Teachers pursue independent learning whilst engaging in collaborative inquiry with peers and participating in professional learning communities.

(b) **Practice-based reflection:** Educators reflect on their classroom practice to inform their professional learning.

(c) **Resource access and formal learning:** Teachers access curated knowledge artefacts, sign up for courses, and track their completion.

9. MOE is exploring the adoption of a professional learning platform that will enable educators to discover relevant learning resources, participate in collaborative learning activities, and receive contextualised support for their learning and its practical application.

10. MOE is particularly interested in platforms capable of operating in large-scale organisational knowledge environments (estimated up to 45,000 users), where learning artefacts and resources may be distributed across multiple repositories. The platform must support secure retrieval, discovery, and synthesis of knowledge while respecting repository-level access controls and user permissions. The platform is intended to support educators across schools and HQ divisions, with the potential for phased rollout across the education system.

---

## PLATFORM OUTCOMES

11. Educators using the platform should be able to achieve the following outcomes:

(a) **Learning content discovery and administration:** Discover relevant professional learning resources efficiently through intelligent search and synthesised recommendations, and engage in independent professional learning through curated microlearning modules and learning pathways.

(b) **Collaboration and Community:** Collaborate with peers and communities of practice through discussion, artefact sharing, feedback, and reflection.

(c) **AI-Enhanced Support:** Receive AI-enhanced support in discovering relevant research, practices, and resources tailored to their needs, as well as personalised learning recommendations and support for creation of learning materials.

(d) **Knowledge Management at Scale:** Support synthesis, retrieval, and organisation of a growing knowledge base of professional artefacts — including documents, videos, images, and collaborative outputs — while remaining performant at scale.

Additional outcomes may also be considered to enhance the effectiveness, adoption, and long-term sustainability of the platform.

17. MOE is particularly interested in mobile-friendly solutions that support semantic knowledge discovery, synthesis across multiple knowledge sources, and dynamic learning pathways, rather than platforms that primarily deliver static course content.
